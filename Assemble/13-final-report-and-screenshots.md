## Screenshot capture protocol

Use synthetic identities on a reviewed local or staging environment. Record the actual build SHA, route, role, viewport, theme and capture date after capture. Retain originals privately if needed; commit screenshots only after privacy review. The seven existing screenshots are historical captures of the real application, not evidence of current public hosting.

Capture the homepage/club search; login with blank credential fields; student dashboard and pending approval; approved community; training/tournament registration; booking request/history; admin CRUD, approvals, reports and results; mobile layout and both themes. For 2FA show the account-security enabled status only: **never show the key, code, recovery dialog or network headers**. Redact personal records without changing what the screenshot claims to demonstrate. Do not manufacture browser output or present concept mockups as real application captures.
